# Relationship Health Check

*Use the M.E.N.T.O.R. framework from One Minute Mentoring to diagnose and strengthen your mentorship.*

---

## When to Use This

- At your midpoint checkpoint (per your Relationship Agreement)
- When something feels "off" in the relationship
- Before deciding whether to renew the relationship
- Quarterly for ongoing mentorships

---

## Sunset Clause Check

*From your Relationship Agreement*

- **Original end date**: [Date]
- **Today's date**: [Date]
- **Time remaining**: [X weeks/months]

### Decision Point

Based on this health check, should we:

- [ ] **Continue as planned** - Relationship is healthy
- [ ] **Adjust and continue** - Address the issues identified above
- [ ] **Extend with new goals** - We've achieved our goals but want to continue
- [ ] **Conclude gracefully** - We've accomplished what we set out to do
- [ ] **End early** - The relationship isn't working

### If Concluding

- What did we accomplish together?
- What did the mentee learn?
- What did the mentor learn?
- Would we recommend each other as mentor/mentee?
- Any final advice or connections to share?
