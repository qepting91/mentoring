# Certification Tracker

*Monitor your progress toward professional certifications and connect them to your goals.*

## Why Certifications Matter

Certifications can:
- Validate skills to employers
- Force structured learning
- Open doors to new roles
- Increase earning potential
- Build confidence

**But:** Not all certifications are created equal. Discuss with your mentor which ones matter in your industry.

## Certification Study Tips

From mentors and successful candidates:

| Tip | Details |
|-----|---------|
| Set a deadline | Book the exam first to create urgency |
| Practice exams | Take multiple practice tests under exam conditions |
| Hands-on labs | Theory isn't enough for technical certs |
| Study groups | Find others preparing for the same cert |
| Teach others | Explaining concepts reveals gaps |
| Spaced repetition | Review regularly, not just before exam |
