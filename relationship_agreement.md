## Mentor Commitments

Based on *The Coaching Habit* principles:

- [ ] I will stay curious longer and rush to advice slower
- [ ] I will ask "And What Else?" before offering solutions
- [ ] I will help you find your own answers, not give you mine
- [ ] I will be honest, even when it's uncomfortable
- [ ] I will respect your time and come prepared
- [ ] I will [Add personal commitment]

---

## Mentee Commitments

Based on *One Minute Mentoring* and *Lean In* principles:

- [ ] I will drive this relationship and own the agenda
- [ ] I will never ask "What should I do?" without first sharing what I've tried
- [ ] I will report back on how I applied previous guidance (ROE)
- [ ] I will be specific about challenges, not vague about problems
- [ ] I will do the work between meetings
- [ ] I will [Add personal commitment]
