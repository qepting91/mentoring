# Self-Evaluation

*Honest self-assessment is the foundation of growth. Complete this before requesting mentor feedback.*

---

## How to Use This Document

1. **Complete honestly** - This is for your benefit, not to impress anyone
2. **Be specific** - Vague assessments lead to vague improvements
3. **Share with your mentor** - Use this as a conversation starter
4. **Compare to mentor feedback** - Notice gaps between your perception and theirs
