# Mentee Feedback to Mentor

*Healthy mentorship relationships include bi-directional feedback.*

---

## Why This Matters

From *Radical Candor*: Care Personally + Challenge Directly = Growth for both parties.

Your mentor invests time and energy in you. Thoughtful feedback helps them become a better mentor - for you and for others.

---

## When to Give Feedback

| Timing | Type of Feedback |
|--------|------------------|
| After each meeting | Quick pulse check (optional) |
| Monthly | Brief reflection on what's working |
| Quarterly | Structured feedback session |
| At relationship end | Comprehensive review |

---

## Ground Rules

- **Be specific**: "When you did X, it helped me because Y" not "You're great"
- **Be timely**: Share feedback close to when it happened
- **Be constructive**: Focus on impact, not judgment
- **Be grateful**: Acknowledge the time and effort they invest

## How to Deliver This Feedback

### Option 1: Written First, Then Discuss

1. Complete this document
2. Share with your mentor before your meeting
3. Discuss together
4. Agree on any changes

### Option 2: Live Conversation

1. Use this as a discussion guide
2. Take notes together
3. Summarize agreements in writing

### Conversation Starters

- "I wanted to share some thoughts on how our mentorship is going..."
- "I've been reflecting on what's working well and what could be even better..."
- "I really appreciate [specific thing]. One area I'd love to explore is..."
