## The Foundation Question

Before defining specific goals, reflect deeply:

**What do you actually want from your career?**
> [Write your honest answer - not what you think you should want]

**Why do you want this?**
> [Dig into the real motivation]

**What will be different when you achieve this?**
> [Paint a concrete picture]
