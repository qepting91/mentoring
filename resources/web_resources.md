## Career Development

### Salary Negotiation

- **Type**: Article
- **Author**: Patrick McKenzie (patio11)
- **Link**: [Salary Negotiation](https://www.kalzumeus.com/2012/01/23/salary-negotiation/)
- **Summary**: You should always negotiate your salary because it has a massive impact on your lifetime earnings and employers typically expect it. Since your salary is only a small fraction of a company's total hiring costs, asking for more is rarely a deal-breaker for them. The golden rule is to never give a number first; always make the company name a figure to avoid underselling yourself. Approach the conversation as a professional business transaction about the value you provide, not as a personal conflict.
- **Key Takeaways**:
  - Always negotiate - it's expected and rarely costs you the offer
  - Never give the first number
  - Frame it as business value, not personal need
- **Best For**: Anyone preparing for job offers or salary reviews

### Job Hunt Like a Hacker

- **Type**: Video
- **Author**: Jason Blanchard
- **Link**: [Job Hunt Like a Hacker](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7gjxaztFxuE)
- **Summary**: The traditional way of job hunting—waiting until you are unemployed and then writing a resume from scratch—is broken and anxiety-inducing. Instead, adopt a proactive, continuous approach where you treat job hunting like a daily habit (10–15 minutes) even when you are happily employed.
- **Key Strategies**:
  - **"Harvest" Your Resume**: Search for job descriptions of roles you want, copy bullet points that are true about you, and use those exact industry terms
  - **Don't Disqualify Yourself**: You don't need 100% of the skills to apply. Categorize skills into:
    - *Familiar With*: You've heard of it, researched it, or took a class
    - *Proficient In*: You use it daily and know the shortcuts
    - *Expert In*: You know it well enough to teach someone else
  - **The "Student" Outreach Hack**: Connect with employees (not just recruiters) at target companies on LinkedIn. Ask for a 15-minute chat: "How did you get to where you are, and what advice do you have for someone like me?"
  - **Crucial Follow-up**: Share your top two takeaways and promise to follow up in a few months to share how you applied their advice. This builds genuine mentor relationships that often lead to job offers.
- **Best For**: Job seekers and those wanting to build their professional network proactively
