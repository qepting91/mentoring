# Recommended Books for Mentoring

A curated list focused on evidence-based mentoring practices and frameworks.

---

## Core Framework Books

*These four books form the foundation of effective mentorship practice.*

### *The Coaching Habit: Say Less, Ask More & Change the Way You Lead Forever*

- **Author**: Michael Bungay Stanier
- **Why It's Essential**: Introduces the seven essential questions that transform mentoring conversations. Teaches mentors to resist the "Advice Monster" and stay curious longer.
- **Key Concepts**:
  - The AWE Question: "And What Else?"
  - The Focus Question: "What is the real challenge here for you?"
  - The Foundation Question: "What do you want?"
  - The Lazy Question: "How can I help?"
  - The Learning Question: "What was most useful for you?"
- **Best For**: Mentors who give too much advice too quickly
- **Link**: [The Coaching Habit](https://www.amazon.com/Coaching-Habit-Less-Change-Forever/dp/0978440749)

---

### *One Minute Mentoring: How to Find and Work with a Mentor – and Why You'll Benefit from Being One*

- **Author**: Ken Blanchard, Claire Diaz-Ortiz
- **Why It's Essential**: Provides the structural framework for mentorship relationships, including how to start, maintain, and conclude them effectively.
- **Key Concepts**:
  - The M.E.N.T.O.R. model (Mission, Engagement, Networking, Trust, Opportunity, Review)
  - Return on Energy (ROE) - reporting back on applied advice
  - The Sunset Clause - defining relationship endpoints
  - Mentee-driven relationships
- **Best For**: Anyone starting a new mentorship (mentor or mentee)
- **Link**: [One Minute Mentoring](https://www.amazon.com/One-Minute-Mentoring-Work-Mentor/dp/0062429302)

---

### *Lean In: Women, Work, and the Will to Lead*

- **Author**: Sheryl Sandberg
- **Why It's Essential**: Challenges mentees to take ownership and "sit at the table." Distinguishes between mentors (advice-givers) and sponsors (advocates).
- **Key Concepts**:
  - "Sit at the table" - active participation in your own development
  - Mentor vs. Sponsor distinction
  - Don't wait to be asked - take initiative
  - The importance of internal confidence alongside external action
- **Best For**: Mentees who are passive or waiting for opportunities
- **Link**: [Lean In](https://www.amazon.com/Lean-Women-Work-Will-Lead/dp/0385349947)

---

### *Tribe of Mentors: Short Life Advice from the Best in the World*

- **Author**: Tim Ferriss
- **Why It's Essential**: Demonstrates that mentorship can come from many sources. Provides a template of powerful questions for self-reflection and mentor conversations.
- **Key Concepts**:
  - The 11 Questions framework for gaining wisdom
  - Multiple mentors for different life areas
  - Learning from failures, not just successes
  - The power of specific, well-crafted questions
- **Key Questions from the Book**:
  1. What is the book you've given most as a gift, and why?
  2. What purchase of $100 or less has most positively impacted your life?
  3. How has a failure, or apparent failure, set you up for later success?
  4. What is one of the best investments you've ever made?
  5. What is an unusual habit or absurd thing that you love?
  6. What advice would you give to a smart, driven college student?
  7. What are bad recommendations you hear in your profession?
  8. When you feel overwhelmed or unfocused, what do you do?
- **Best For**: Expanding sources of wisdom beyond a single mentor
- **Link**: [Tribe of Mentors](https://www.amazon.com/Tribe-Mentors-Short-Advice-World/dp/1328994961)

## Additional Recommended Books

### *The Mentoring Manual: Your Step-by-Step Guide to Being a Better Mentor*

- **Author**: Julie Starr
- **Summary**: Practical guide with frameworks, tools, and strategies for developing the right mindset and approaches to building productive mentor-mentee relationships.
- **Best For**: New mentors wanting a structured approach
- **Link**: [The Mentoring Manual](https://www.amazon.com/Mentoring-Manual-Step-Step-Better/dp/1292017899)

---

### *Radical Candor: Be a Kick-Ass Boss Without Losing Your Humanity*

- **Author**: Kim Scott
- **Summary**: Explores honest, direct communication in leadership and mentoring. Teaches how to provide feedback that is both effective and compassionate.
- **Key Concepts**:
  - Care Personally + Challenge Directly = Radical Candor
  - Avoiding Ruinous Empathy (caring but not challenging)
  - Avoiding Obnoxious Aggression (challenging without caring)
- **Best For**: Mentors struggling to give honest feedback
- **Link**: [Radical Candor](https://www.amazon.com/Radical-Candor-Kick-Ass-Without-Humanity/dp/1250235375)

---

### *Dare to Lead: Brave Work. Tough Conversations. Whole Hearts.*

- **Author**: Brené Brown
- **Summary**: Leadership and mentorship through vulnerability and courage. Focuses on building trust, empathy, and resilience.
- **Key Concepts**:
  - Vulnerability as strength, not weakness
  - BRAVING Trust framework
  - The courage to have difficult conversations
- **Best For**: Building deeper trust in mentoring relationships
- **Link**: [Dare to Lead](https://www.amazon.com/Dare-Lead-Brave-Conversations-Hearts/dp/0399592520)

---

### *Mentor: The Kid and the CEO*

- **Author**: Tom Pace, Walter Jenkins
- **Summary**: A fictional story based on real-life experiences exploring the transformative power of mentoring relationships.
- **Best For**: Understanding the human impact of mentoring
- **Link**: [Mentor: The Kid and the CEO](https://www.amazon.com/Mentor-Kid-CEO-Tom-Pace/dp/0979396247)

---

### *Mentoring 101: What Every Leader Needs to Know*

- **Author**: John C. Maxwell
- **Summary**: Concise guide focusing on key elements of successful mentoring, emphasizing the role of mentoring in leadership development.
- **Best For**: Leaders looking to invest in the growth of others
- **Link**: [Mentoring 101](https://www.amazon.com/Mentoring-101-John-C-Maxwell/dp/1400280222)

---

### *Everyone Deserves a Great Manager: The 6 Critical Practices for Leading a Team*

- **Author**: Scott Miller, Todd Davis, Victoria Roos Olsson
- **Summary**: Six critical practices applicable to mentoring, including building trust, developing talent, and leading with purpose.
- **Best For**: Managers who also serve as mentors
- **Link**: [Everyone Deserves a Great Manager](https://www.amazon.com/Everyone-Deserves-Great-Manager-Practices/dp/1982112077)

---

### *Mastering Mentorship: A Practical Guide for Mentors and Mentees*

- **Author**: Julie Thompson
- **Summary**: Covers the mentoring process from start to finish with focus on setting clear goals and overcoming challenges.
- **Best For**: Structured, goal-oriented mentoring
- **Link**: [Mastering Mentorship](https://uk.sagepub.com/en-gb/eur/mastering-mentorship/book236028)

## Reading Progression

| Stage | Book | Why Now |
|-------|------|---------|
| **Before starting** | One Minute Mentoring | Understand relationship structure |
| **First 30 days** | The Coaching Habit | Learn the core questions |
| **If you're a mentee** | Lean In | Take ownership of your development |
| **For deeper wisdom** | Tribe of Mentors | Expand your perspective |
| **For feedback skills** | Radical Candor | Learn to be honestly helpful |
