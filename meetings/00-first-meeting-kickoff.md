# First Meeting: Relationship Kickoff

**Date**: [YYYY-MM-DD]
**Attendees**: [Mentor Name], [Mentee Name]
**Duration**: 60-90 minutes (longer than regular meetings)

---

## Purpose

This is not a regular mentorship meeting. The goal is to:
1. Get to know each other beyond titles and roles
2. Establish the relationship foundation
3. Complete the [Relationship Agreement](../relationship_agreement.md)
4. Set clear expectations for both parties

---

## Pre-Meeting Preparation

### For the Mentee
- [ ] Write a 1-page "About Me" covering: career journey, current role, aspirations
- [ ] Identify 3 specific areas where you want to grow
- [ ] Prepare 2-3 questions about the mentor's journey
- [ ] Review the Relationship Agreement template

### For the Mentor
- [ ] Reflect on what you can realistically offer
- [ ] Consider your mentoring style and boundaries
- [ ] Prepare to share relevant parts of your own journey
- [ ] Review the Relationship Agreement template

---

## Meeting Agenda

### Part 1: Connection (20 min)

#### Mentee Shares
- Career journey: How did you get here?
- Current reality: What does your day-to-day look like?
- What energizes you about your work?
- What drains you?

#### Mentor Shares
- Relevant parts of your journey
- Lessons learned (including failures)
- Why you mentor

---

### Part 2: The Foundation Question (15 min)

*From The Coaching Habit: "What do you want?"*

**Mentee**: Take 5 minutes to answer each of these:

1. **What do you want from your career in the next 1-2 years?**

   [Response]

2. **What do you want from this mentorship specifically?**

   [Response]

3. **What would make you say "this was worth my time" in 6 months?**

   [Response]

**Mentor**: Reflect back what you heard. Ask clarifying questions.

---

### Part 3: Expectation Alignment (15 min)

#### Discuss Together

**Mentor's Role**
- [ ] Sounding board (listen and reflect)
- [ ] Advisor (share experience and perspective)
- [ ] Connector (make introductions)
- [ ] Sponsor (advocate publicly)
- [ ] Coach (ask questions to develop thinking)
- [ ] Teacher (direct skill transfer)

*Note: Mentor is likely NOT all of these. Clarify which ones.*

**What Mentor Cannot Provide**
- [Be explicit about limitations]

**Mentee's Responsibilities**
- Drive the relationship
- Prepare for meetings
- Follow through on commitments
- Report back on outcomes (ROE)

---

### Part 4: Complete the Relationship Agreement (20 min)

Open [relationship_agreement.md](../relationship_agreement.md) and complete together:

- [ ] Sunset clause dates
- [ ] Meeting cadence
- [ ] Communication boundaries
- [ ] Confidentiality terms
- [ ] Success metrics
