## Part 3: The AWE Question

*From The Coaching Habit: "And What Else?"*

After discussing the initial topic, go deeper:

**Mentor asks**: "And what else is on your mind about this?"

[Response]

**Mentor asks again**: "And what else?"

[Response]

*Repeat until you've uncovered the real issue. Usually takes 3-4 rounds.*

## Part 6: The Lazy Question

*From The Coaching Habit: "How can I help?"*

**Mentor asks**: "How can I help?" or "What do you want from me?"

**Mentee**: Be specific. Not "any advice?" but rather:

- [ ] I want an introduction to [Person/Network]
- [ ] I want you to challenge my thinking on [Y]
- [ ] I want you to share your experience with [Z]
