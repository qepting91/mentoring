## The Event ROI Framework

**No strategy = No value.** Before any event, answer:

1. **Why am I going?** (Specific goal, not "to network")
2. **Who do I want to meet?** (Names or types of people)
3. **What will I do after?** (Follow-up plan)

## Event Planning with Your Mentor

Before attending events, discuss:
- What do I want to get out of this?
- Who should I try to meet?
- What questions should I ask?
- How does this connect to my goals?

After events, report back (ROE):
- What did I learn?
- Who did I meet?
- What will I do differently?

## Post-Event Follow-up Checklist

Complete within 48 hours of the event:

- [ ] Sent personalized follow-up to each meaningful connection
- [ ] Connected on LinkedIn with personalized message
- [ ] Added new contacts to contacts.md
- [ ] Scheduled any promised follow-up meetings
- [ ] Shared key learnings with mentor
- [ ] Updated this document with outcomes
- [ ] Thanked mentor for any pre-event guidance

**Follow-up Message Template:**

> Hi [Name],
>
> It was great meeting you at [Event] yesterday. I enjoyed our conversation about [specific topic you discussed].
>
> [Optional: Share something relevant - article, resource, introduction offer]
>
> I'd love to stay connected. [Optional: Suggest specific next step]
>
> Best,
> [Your name]
