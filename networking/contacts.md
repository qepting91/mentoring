# Professional Network

*From M.E.N.T.O.R. framework: Networking is a core component of mentorship value.*

---

## How to Use This Document

1. Track connections made through your mentorship
2. Note who your mentor could introduce you to
3. Record introductions made and their outcomes
4. Build your own "Tribe of Mentors"
5. Maintain relationships with regular touchpoints

---

## The Golden Rule of Networking

**No follow-up = No value.** Every connection is an investment. Protect that investment by:
- Following up within 48 hours of meeting someone
- Providing value before asking for anything
- Maintaining regular contact based on relationship tier

---

## Relationship Tiers & Follow-up Frequency

| Tier | Who | Follow-up Frequency | Touchpoint Ideas |
|------|-----|---------------------|------------------|
| **Inner Circle** | Primary mentors, sponsors, key allies | Monthly | Coffee, video call, meaningful message |
| **Active Network** | Colleagues, peers you work with regularly | Quarterly | Share article, congratulate, quick check-in |
| **Warm Connections** | Met once, positive interaction, potential | Every 6 months | Holiday message, career update, share opportunity |
| **Dormant** | Haven't connected in 12+ months | Annually or re-activate | "Thinking of you" message, life update |

## Value-First Touchpoint Ideas

When reaching out, lead with value:

| Situation | Touchpoint Idea |
|-----------|-----------------|
| They got promoted | Congratulations message (be specific about why they deserve it) |
| Their company in news | Share article with brief insight |
| You learned something relevant | "Thought of you when I read/heard this..." |
| You can make an intro | Offer to connect them with someone helpful |
| Annual check-in | Career update + genuine question about them |
| Holiday/New Year | Brief, personalized message (not mass email) |

**Never:** Reach out only when you need something.
